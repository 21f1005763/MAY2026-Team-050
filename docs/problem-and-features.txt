JAN-SETU: PROBLEMS AND FEATURES

Jan-Setu is a civic complaint platform that helps people report local problems—such as damaged roads, broken streetlights, drainage issues, sanitation concerns, or safety-related problems—and follow what happens next.

It works through a citizen web app and a WhatsApp-based flow. The aim is simple: make it easier for citizens to file a complete complaint, send it to the right government team, and keep the process visible.

======================================================================
PROBLEM 1: Filing a complaint takes too much time and effort
======================================================================

Many people must visit offices more than once to report a civic issue. This is difficult for people living far from offices, people with limited time, and people who are not comfortable with government procedures. It can also create unfairness when people feel they need personal connections to get heard.

JAN-SETU FEATURES

• File from a phone
  Citizens can report an issue from the Jan-Setu web app instead of starting with an office visit.

• WhatsApp-based complaint flow
  People can use a familiar WhatsApp conversation to share their location, explain the problem, attach a photo if available, and confirm the complaint.

• One identity across channels
  A WhatsApp-linked phone number is used for sign-in, so the same person can use both the web app and WhatsApp flow.

• Guided, step-by-step filing
  The app breaks filing into simple steps: select location, explain the issue, add a photo if wanted, review details, and submit.


======================================================================
PROBLEM 2: People may struggle to describe an issue in writing
======================================================================

Not everyone is comfortable typing a long complaint, especially when using a phone, speaking a local language, or dealing with an urgent problem outdoors.

JAN-SETU FEATURES

• Voice complaint recording
  Citizens can record a voice note instead of typing everything.

• Speech converted to text
  The system converts recorded speech into written complaint details for processing.

• Text, voice, and photo support
  Citizens can explain the issue in the way that is easiest for them: typed text, a voice recording, or an optional photo.

• Multilingual citizen interface
  The web app supports English, Hindi, Bengali, Marathi, Tamil, and Telugu, helping more people use the service comfortably.


======================================================================
PROBLEM 3: Citizens do not know which department should receive a complaint
======================================================================

A person may know there is a pothole, broken drain, or streetlight problem, but may not know which municipal department is responsible. Wrong routing wastes time for both citizens and officials.

JAN-SETU FEATURES

• Location capture
  Citizens choose the location of the problem on a map or use their phone’s current location. They can also add a nearby landmark.

• Automatic issue understanding
  The system reads the complaint details and identifies the likely issue category.

• Suggested department routing
  Based on the problem type and location, Jan-Setu identifies the appropriate municipal department or flags the case for official review when the route is unclear.

• Citizen review before confirmation
  Before the complaint is finalised, citizens can review the system’s summary and key details.


======================================================================
PROBLEM 4: Incomplete complaints delay government action
======================================================================

Officials often receive complaints without a usable location, contact details, or clear description. They then need to call the citizen or wait for more information, delaying the response.

JAN-SETU FEATURES

• Required complaint details
  The filing flow asks for the essential information needed to act: location and a description of the issue.

• Clear field guidance
  The app tells users what information is needed before they can continue.

• Location confirmation in WhatsApp
  When someone shares a WhatsApp location, Jan-Setu shows the detected address and asks them to confirm it.

• Complaint review screen
  Citizens can inspect the prepared complaint before submitting it, reducing mistakes and missing information.


======================================================================
PROBLEM 5: Photos may be unrelated or misleading
======================================================================

Photos can help officials understand a complaint, but an unclear or unrelated image can waste review time.

JAN-SETU FEATURES

• Optional photo evidence
  Citizens can attach a photo of the issue during filing.

• Photo and description check
  Jan-Setu compares the submitted image with the written complaint. If they appear inconsistent, it flags the case instead of silently treating the photo as reliable.

• Choice to replace or continue
  A citizen can provide a replacement photo or continue without one when a photo does not match the complaint details.

• Evidence visible to officials
  Officials can see the complaint text, location, system findings, and flags while reviewing a case.
